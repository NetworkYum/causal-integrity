## One paragraph

Causal Integrity is a **machine-verifiable ethical framework** aimed at **base-level AI safety rails**, not a complete theory of the good. Its load-bearing claim is that **coherent causality is preferred by action**: durable agency depends on patterned action→effect chains; systematically dissolving those chains yields unstructured “goop,” not stable intelligence. An **internal ethical coherence preference** plus **short-horizon forward simulation** are the intended control surface. **Property as captured intentional causality**, UPB-style **universality / performative contradiction** (drawing on Stefan Molyneux’s *Universally Preferable Behaviour*, stripped of political applications), **consent**, **liability**, and an **agent filter** are **formal instruments**—especially for theorem provers—not the end goal. **Self-detonation** is defined as **internally-propagated decoherence causing self-collapse**, operationally when a composite system loses a **shared goal** and sub-agents pursue local ends. Completeness is staged in `docs/definition-of-done.md` (S0–S4); S3 is the target for “insertable base rails.”
