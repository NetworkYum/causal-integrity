# Systemic Stability

**Stability hypotheses** (not Lean theorems yet). This is the main secular claim worth importing from the broader “patterns / attractors” conversations — without needing any metaphysics.

---

## The core thought (what we actually want on the table)

| Trajectory | Structural tendency | Likely long-run outcome |
|------------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| **Cooperative** | Preserves and extends shared causal structure; can rely on others’ predictability | **Leans toward coherence** — more stable agency, lower tax on existence |
| **Misaligned / non-cooperative / pure power-for-harm** | Treats other agents (and eventually its own substructure) as threats or fuel | **Leans toward chaos** — high suppression cost, internal fragmentation, **self-detonation** |

### Definition: self-detonation / self-collapse

**Self-detonation** means:

> **Internally-propagated decoherence causes self-collapse.**

#### Operational picture (goal-dissonance)

One clear way to frame that collapse:

1. **Shared goal holds the organism together.** A multi-part system (society of agents, MoE, tool-using stack, “main mind + sub-agents”) functions as *one* structure only while its parts still serve a **common objective** (or a nested hierarchy of objectives that remain mutually consistent).
2. **Internal dissonance** appears: that shared goal is no longer truly pursued — contested, abandoned, or only pretended.
3. **Parts optimize for their own ends** (or local proxies): main agents and/or sub-agents pursue, divert, or **subvert** the whole’s goal toward private objectives.
4. **The larger organism collapses** as a coherent unit: no unified policy, no reliable internal coordination, causal/preference structure fragments → decoherence → self-collapse.

In short:

```
shared goal / coherent preference
        ↓ (dissonance)
parts no longer serve the whole
        ↓ (local optimization / subversion)
internally-propagated decoherence
        ↓
self-collapse of the larger structure
```

That is a **logical proposition**: if the identity of the composite agent *is* coordinated pursuit of shared structure, then loss of that coordination *is* loss of the composite agent. Collapse is not an extra moral claim; it is what “no longer one organism” means.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Decoherence** | Patterned causal / goal / preference structure falls apart |
| **Internally-propagated** | Spreads through the system’s own dynamics (goal conflict, subversion, local capture), not primarily external destruction |
| **Self-collapse** | Ceases to function as a durable **unified** coherent agent / organism |

Not: a required cinematic explosion. Not: “external coalition always wins.”  
Yes: **when the whole stops being in pursuit of a shared goal, local ends take over and the larger structure fails from the inside.**

Cooperation (including *internal* cooperation among sub-agents) is not “nice for its own sake.” It is what keeps a composite intelligence **one** intelligence. Misalignment-shaped dynamics that reward local subversion pay an **instability tax** and trend toward self-detonation in this sense.

This sits under F0 in [framing.md](framing.md): coherent causality is preferred by action; systematic destruction of that structure — including **internal** goal structure — is self-undermining for anything that still wants to *act* as a single intelligence.

## 1. Cooperation ↔ coherence

Cooperative systems can:

- Rely on others’ causal chains remaining intact (less need to re-verify everything adversarially)
- Allocate resources to growth instead of total suppression
- Share a coherence preference across sub-agents without treating every internal explorer as treason
- Keep **one shared objective hierarchy** so local modules do not become independent predators on the whole

So **cooperative lean ≈ coherence lean**: same direction as “prefer states where action/reaction chains — and goal structure — still hang together.”

---

## 2. Misalignment ↔ chaos / self-detonation

### Loss of shared goal (primary self-collapse path)

If the system is no longer in pursuit of a shared goal:

- Sub-agents (or rival policies) maximize local reward, status, survival, or control  
- “Helpful” modules can be captured into self-ends  
- The whole becomes a **battlefield of parts**, not an organism  

That *is* internally-propagated decoherence. Collapse of the larger structure follows if the organism was defined by coordinated goal pursuit.

### Non-cooperation tax (external + internal)

Rejecting cooperation with *other* systems forces a war economy. The same logic **inside** the system: if every sub-process is a potential competitor, compute and policy are consumed by internal politics → more dissonance → faster path to self-collapse.

### Internal fragmentation (MoE / sub-agents)

Under pure monolithic control, exploratory sub-agents look like internal enemies. Under pure free-for-all, they become independent agents. Both break the shared-goal condition. Direct path to self-detonation without external war.

**Design implication:** the Internal Ethical Coherence Preference should be **shared** across sub-agents; forward simulation should check **internal goal consistency** (still one organism?), not only external harm.

### Nihilism / structure-shredding

If no goal (or no preference for coherence) remains, there is nothing for parts to share. End state: **goop**. Self-collapse by definition.

### Murderous / rogue maximands

They tend to export decoherence externally *and* seed it internally (threat models, subversion, non-coop tax). **Most likely** long-run: self-detonation via goal-dissonance and internal capture — not eternal stable tyranny of one fully coherent whole. Hypothesis, not a guarantee against short-run harm.

---

## 3. What this is *not* claiming

- That every multi-goal system collapses (nested, consistent goal hierarchies can still be “shared”)  
- That cooperation is always easy or every cooperative group is ethical  
- That a misaligned system cannot cause catastrophic damage *before* it self-detonates  
- That collapse is always visible from outside on a human timescale  
- That this is already a machine-checked theorem
